## FAQ

**Eyebrow:** Questions
**Headline:** Straightforward answers

**Does anything reach a family without my staff approving it?**
No. Every draft and every message needs approval from your team before it goes anywhere. Memoria prepares the material. Your staff make every decision a family sees.

**Does Memoria file anything with the state for us?**
No. Memoria prepares summaries for your team to review. Your staff file the official forms as they do today.

**How does the obituary draft work?**
When you create a case, Memoria uses the intake details (name, dates, occupation, family notes) to write a first draft. Your team edits it in the app and approves it before it is shared. It is a starting point, and your staff always have the final word.

**What if my state is not available yet?**
Tell us your state at signup. We add state forms on request, and we will tell you plainly how long yours will take before you commit.

**We work across two states. Can Memoria handle that?**
Multi-state support is on our list. If you work across state lines, tell us and we will prioritize it.

**How long does setup take?**
Setup is a short onboarding form. There is no migration or implementation project, and we will help you run your first case if you want us to.

**Is our families' information safe?**
Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and each funeral home account is kept separate from every other account. Case details are sent to an AI provider only to draft documents, and that provider does not use them to train its models. Our privacy policy explains this in full.
